Digital Council Services Platform - working prototype (fictional data)
=====================================================================

HOW TO OPEN
1. Unzip this folder.
2. Double-click start.html (opens in your web browser: Chrome, Edge or Firefox).
3. Use the dark "Demo view" bar at the top to switch between:
   Citizen · Health centre · Registrar · Treasurer · Field agent · Administration ·
   Management · All councils · How it works · Demo guide · Coverage

No internet connection or installation is needed. Everything you do is saved only in your
own browser; "Reset demo data" (top right) restores the original data.

MENU LAYOUT
- In Administration › Look and feel, each council chooses a horizontal (top) or vertical (left side)
  menu for its website and staff screens. Only that council changes; phones and the mobile app keep
  their own menus.

FUNDER ALIGNMENT (Cities Deliver: The Rockefeller Foundation and the OpenAI Foundation)
- All data is synthetic and fictional; no personal data is used, as the funder requires
  for application materials and demonstrations.
- AI answers are labelled as AI; staff approve every legal act.
- The platform is city-led: each council joins through its own council and letter of intent.
- Licensed under the Apache License 2.0 (see LICENSE), as the funder's open-source rule requires.

IMPORTANT
All names, numbers, fees and statistics are fictional and for discussion only.
The assistant is a rule-based demo of how answers will work; mobile money, SMS, USSD
and BUNEC connections are simulated. This is not an official council service.
