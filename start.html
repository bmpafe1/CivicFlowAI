Guided journeys (5–10 minutes each)

A. A birth in a health centre

  1. Health centre: open "Notify a birth" and press Send. Try "Simulate no network" first to see offline saving.
  2. Follow the link to the parent's screen and press "Everything is correct".
  3. Open the registrar queue, select the new request, read the AI pre-check, press "Approve and sign".
  4. Back on the citizen side, track the request and open the certificate with its QR code, then check it on Verify.

B. Late registration with the AI assistant, in Pidgin and by voice

  1. Open the assistant and press the yellow microphone button (a demo voice note in Pidgin is used). Press "Listen" to hear the answer.
  2. Press "Start the request", answer the questions, add photos of documents (any image), choose an appointment and submit.
  3. In the registrar queue, the request shows what is missing. Approve it, then pay it from the citizen's tracking page with mobile money (any 4-digit PIN).

C. A lost certificate, from another town, in French

  1. Switch to FR (top right) and ask the assistant: "J'ai perdu l'acte de naissance de mon fils pendant la crise".
  2. See request CP-2026-00890, found by the AI in the digitised 2009 register; then approve it as the registrar.
  3. Open "Register digitisation" in the registrar view to see how old handwritten registers are captured.

C2. Lost certificate: instant, manual or another council

  1. Open Find my certificate and try the three examples: Marie Ngole 2009 (digitised: pay and download at once), Clovis Ayuk 2004 (not yet scanned: pay, then wait about 3 days), Mary Ebot Ashu 1998 (found at other councils).
  2. For Clovis Ayuk, go to Archives › Retrieval requests, upload any image as the scanned page, check the AI reading and issue. Track the request as the citizen: it is ready to download. Search Clovis Ayuk again: it is now instant.
  3. Explore the scan station, archive search and AI analysis in Archives.

D. Moto-taxi rider and anti-extortion

  1. On Riders, look up MR-0937 (valid) and MR-1022 (expired); buy a jacket online.
  2. Send an anonymous report of an illegal demand, then see it on the management dashboard.
  3. Ask the assistant about the same situation.

E. A new council joins

  1. On the platform console, create "Kumba II Council".
  2. Choose it in the council selector of the citizen, registrar or management view, or choose "Commune de Douala III" to see a French-speaking council.

F. Citizen learning and training

  1. Open Learn & jobs, start "Why and how to register a birth on time", press Listen on a lesson and answer the quick check.
  2. Open "Training, online learning and careers" and book a place at the computer skills session or the career fair; see the course badge in My account.
  3. Health workers: "For health workers" tab, also linked from the health centre portal.

G. Running the council

  1. Administration: add a staff member, change a fee (it changes everywhere), try to publish news without French, approve an assistant answer, draft bilingual minutes.
  2. Treasurer: reconcile payments, grant an exemption, approve a refund. Field agent: scan a badge or licence.
  3. Management: ask a question about your data, send a sensitisation campaign.
  4. Administration › Public page: publish a project, update its progress, add an event or upload a photo, then see it on Our council (follow a project, download an event to your calendar).
  5. Open the Mobile app view in one tab and Administration › Look and feel in another. Pick a theme or your own colours: the website and the phones change at once. Switch to Commune de Douala III to see it keeps its own theme.

Good to know

  • All people, numbers, fees and statistics are fictional and for discussion only. Fees must be confirmed by the council.
  • The assistant here is a rule-based demo of how answers will work. In the real platform, an AI model answers only from the council's verified knowledge base, in English, French and Pidgin, by text and voice.
  • Mobile money, SMS, USSD and BUNEC connections are simulated.
  • "Reset demo data" in the dark bar restores the original data.
