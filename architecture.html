What the AI does, and who checks it

Recent generative models do the tasks rules cannot. The team picks the best model for each task from its own test results; the funder has no provider preference.

TaskWhat the AI doesModels to compareHuman check
Read handwritten registersTurns each scanned entry into typed fields with a confidence scoreHosted multimodal models (OpenAI, Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude); Transkribus handwriting models trained on our registers; open-weight vision models; classic OCR for printed formsClerk verifies low-confidence fields; second-person sample check

Built to the Cities Deliver rules

City-led. Limbe II Council owns the service; each new council joins with its own letter of intent.
Frontier AI at the core. Handwriting, Pidgin voice and conversational forms need generative models.
Responsible AI. Staff approve every legal act; answers are grounded; pass marks before go-live.
Open source. Code, prompts, evaluation tools and documentation under Apache 2.0.
Privacy. Law No. 2024/017 and GDPR principles; minimal data to AI providers; synthetic data in all demos.
No equipment from the grant. Hosting and GPU time are rented; devices come from the council or other funders.
